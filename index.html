Zero-Server Architecture

Your conversations are private.
We keep it that way.

WhatsApp chats contain private moments, banking slips, and sensitive documents. ChatFlow was built on one strict rule: no data ever leaves your browser.

✕

Other Online Parsers

  • ✕ Uploads your raw .txt or .zip to a remote server.
  • ✕ Conversations may be stored in databases or log files.
  • ✕ Exposed to man-in-the-middle attacks and cloud leaks.
✓

ChatFlow Architecture

  • ✓ 100% In-Memory: Parsed inside your browser
  • ✓ Zero Network Requests: No backend APIs, no telemetry, and no storage buckets.
  • ✓ Ephemeral: Closing the tab instantly wipes all parsed data from memory.

Your trust is our foundation

We believe personal memories should stay strictly personal. Nothing is ever saved, shared, or viewed by anyone but you.
